# Warlen Industrial Sales Corporation — HR & Payroll Management System

A web-based platform designed to simplify how businesses manage their employees, track attendance, and process payroll — all in one place.

---

## What It Does

### Employee Management
Keep a complete record of every employee in the company. This includes their personal details, contact information, family background, educational attainment, skills, government ID numbers, and contract information. Each employee has a dedicated profile page where everything is visible at a glance.

### Attendance Tracking
Monitor employee attendance using biometric data imported directly from fingerprint scanners. The system organizes attendance records into four views — daily logs, exception reports (late arrivals, early departures, absences), period summaries, and shift schedules — so managers always know who showed up, when, and for how long.

### Payroll Processing
Calculate employee pay based on actual attendance data. The system accounts for regular hours, overtime, late deductions, and pay frequency (weekly, semi-monthly, or monthly). Employees can also view their own earnings history and performance summary through a personal dashboard.

### Incentives
Assign one-time or daily incentives to individual employees or groups, tied to specific payroll periods. Useful for bonuses, allowances, and performance rewards.

### AI Insights
Receive automatically generated recommendations about workforce attendance patterns, payroll anomalies, and areas of concern — helping managers make informed decisions without manually digging through records.

### Raw File Archive
Every biometric file imported into the system is stored and organized by month, so administrators always have a traceable record of where the data came from.

---

## Who It Is For

- **HR administrators** who manage employee records and contracts
- **Payroll officers** who process and verify employee compensation
- **Managers** who need attendance visibility across departments
- **Employees** who want to track their own earnings and performance

---

## What Problem It Solves

Most small to mid-sized businesses in the Philippines manage payroll through spreadsheets — a process that is slow, error-prone, and difficult to audit. This system replaces that workflow by connecting attendance data directly to payroll computation, keeping employee records in a single organized location, and giving every stakeholder the information they need without back-and-forth communication.
